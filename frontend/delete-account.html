Raptora Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your Raptora account and request deletion of your associated personal data, follow the instructions below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address registered with your Raptora account.
  2. Use the subject: Raptora Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your name and the email address associated with your Raptora account.

Send your request to: raptora247@gmail.com

What data will be deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete your Raptora account and associated personal account information stored by Raptora, where applicable.

Data that may be retained

Certain information, such as transaction, payment, accounting, security, or other records may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. Such information will be retained only for the period required or permitted by applicable law.

Processing of deletion requests

We may verify that the request was made by the account holder before processing the deletion request.

Once the request is verified, we will process the deletion in accordance with our applicable policies and legal obligations.
