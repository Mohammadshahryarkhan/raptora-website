ADMISSION MILESTONES

Understand the general admission journey from entrance examinations and results to counselling, seat allotment, document verification, and finally joining your college.

YOUR ADMISSION JOURNEY

01

Entrance Exam

Register for and appear in the relevant entrance examination required for your chosen course and admission pathway.

02

Results & Rank

Check your examination result, score, percentile, rank, or merit position after the results are announced.

03

Counselling Registration

Register for the appropriate counselling process according to your examination, course, category, and admission authority.

04

Choice Filling

Select your preferred colleges and courses and arrange them according to your priorities.

05

Seat Allotment

Check the result of the counselling round to see whether a college and course has been allotted based on your rank, choices, and available seats.

06

Seat Acceptance

Follow the counselling authority's process to accept, upgrade, freeze, or otherwise proceed with the allotted seat.

07

Document Verification

Submit and verify the required academic, identity, category, and other admission documents.

08

Fee Payment

Pay the required admission or college fees within the specified deadline to complete the next stage of the admission process.

09

College Reporting

Report to the allotted institution within the specified period and complete the required joining formalities.

10

Admission Confirmed

Complete the final admission formalities and officially begin your journey at your chosen college.
