One-to-One Interactions

Sometimes, you don't need another search result. You just need someone to talk to.

Choosing a college, a branch or even a career path can feel confusing.

You might have questions that don't have a simple Google answer. You might want to hear from someone who understands your situation and can actually listen to what you're trying to figure out.

“Sometimes, you just need to talk to someone who gets it.”

What Are One-to-One Interactions?

At RAPTORA, our One-to-One Interactions feature is designed to give students a more personal way to get guidance.

Instead of trying to figure everything out alone, you can share your questions and get guidance based on your individual situation.

What Can You Get Help With?

Your questions can be about almost anything related to your admission journey.

  • Choosing the right college
  • Choosing between different branches
  • Understanding your rank and options
  • College counselling guidance
  • Understanding college cutoffs
  • Fees and affordability
  • Hostel and campus life
  • Placements and internships
  • Career and higher-study decisions
  • General college-related doubts

Google Meet Guidance

Some questions are easier to explain when you're actually talking to someone.

That's why RAPTORA can provide one-to-one guidance sessions through Google Meet.

You can have a proper conversation, explain your situation and ask questions directly instead of sending messages back and forth.

Personal Guidance Session

Discuss your questions directly with a mentor or experienced person through a Google Meet session.

Share your concerns, ask questions and get guidance that is relevant to your situation.

Google Forms for Your Questions

Don't know what to ask or don't have time for a live session immediately?

You can submit your questions through a Google Form.

Tell us about your exam, rank, preferred branch, preferred colleges and what you're confused about. This helps us understand your situation before providing guidance.

It's More Than Just One Answer

One-to-one guidance isn't about simply telling you which college to choose.

It's about understanding your priorities and helping you look at the bigger picture.

Your rank may be good for one college, but another college might offer a better branch for your goals. Your budget might make one option more practical than another.

These are the kinds of things that are easier to discuss when someone actually listens to you.

Live Q&A and Doubt Clearing

Sometimes you don't need a full guidance session. You simply have one question that's bothering you.

Through live Q&A sessions, students can ask questions, hear different perspectives and clear doubts related to admissions and college life.

Guidance That Feels Personal

Every student has a different story.

Different ranks. Different budgets. Different interests. Different goals.

So your guidance shouldn't feel like a generic answer copied from a website.

Your question is personal. Your guidance should be too.

That's What RAPTORA Is Trying to Build

At RAPTORA, we want students to have a place where they can ask questions without feeling lost or overwhelmed.

Whether it's through a Google Meet session, a Google Form, a live Q&A or a simple conversation, the goal is the same:

Help students make clearer and more confident decisions.
